DIGITAL DISTRACTION REACHES CRISIS LEVELS

Across every demographic, daily device usage has climbed to figures once thought impossible. Analysts describe a quiet restructuring of how human beings allocate their waking hours, with consequences only now coming into focus.

Laboratories report measurable declines in sustained focus across age groups, prompting urgent calls for further study and a wider public reckoning with the costs of constant connection.

By J. Morrison, Research Dept.
