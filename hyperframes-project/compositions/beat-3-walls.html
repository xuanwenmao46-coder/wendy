Chapter 2 · The Three Walls

Friendship needs three things
adults rarely have.

01

Proximity

Repeated, unplanned contact is how bonds form. Adult schedules replace serendipity with obligations — killing the accidental encounters that spark friendship.

02

Openness

Vulnerability accelerates closeness. But adults build emotional armour — professionalism, self-sufficiency — that keeps even well-meaning people at arm's length.

03

Repetition

Familiarity builds trust. Adult life is siloed — work, gym, commute — with almost no recurring contexts where you encounter the same new person again and again.
