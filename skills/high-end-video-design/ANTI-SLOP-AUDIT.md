# Anti-Slop Audit

Run this before implementing a HyperFrames clip.

## Immediate Red Flags

- Cyan-on-black plus purple gradient is doing all the design work.
- Product image is smaller than decorative HUD.
- Three or four identical cards appear because the content has three or four features.
- Headline is centered, subtitle is centered, CTA is centered, everything has equal weight.
- Font choice is Arial, Inter, Roboto, Poppins, Sora, Open Sans, Noto Sans, or similar default.
- Text is below 24px in a 1280x720 video.
- Gradient text appears without a strong brand reason.
- Random lines and rings do not point to anything.
- There is no clear light source.

## Fixes

- Replace decorative HUD with one real annotation.
- Make the product or title dramatically larger.
- Use one big feature proof instead of many small cards.
- Move the layout off-center and anchor to an edge.
- Choose one strong display voice and one quiet support voice.
- Use material texture: grain, shadow, matte, reflection, paper, glass, metal.
- Reduce color count to one accent and make it intentional.

## Design Pass Checklist

- [ ] Can the best frame work as a poster?
- [ ] Is there a single obvious hero?
- [ ] Is the second focal point clear?
- [ ] Is the product image respected?
- [ ] Are fonts doing brand work?
- [ ] Are colors limited and assigned roles?
- [ ] Are decorative elements supporting hierarchy?
- [ ] Would this still look good as a still image?
