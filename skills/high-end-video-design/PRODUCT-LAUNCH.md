# Product Launch Design Guidance

## Good Launch Frames

A strong product launch frame usually has:

- One product hero image at dominant scale.
- One title block with real typographic character.
- One proof point: number, quote, feature, or material detail.
- A restrained surface: studio black, paper white, brushed metal, ceramic, glass, or editorial color field.
- A clear light source.

## Better Than HUD

Use these before defaulting to HUD:

- Oversized product crop crossing the frame edge.
- Product reflection or soft floor shadow.
- A single measurement line pointing to a real feature.
- Large editorial type behind the product at low opacity.
- Split-screen material comparison.
- Macro detail inset.
- Number-first proof frame.

## Launch Storyboard Pattern

Use 4 beats for an 8-12 second clip:

1. **Tease**: texture, silhouette, or cropped product detail.
2. **Reveal**: product enters or light reveals form.
3. **Proof**: one feature or metric lands with precision.
4. **Lockup**: product name, slogan, release cue.

## Premium Motion Posture

- Slow reveal beats: 0.8-1.4s.
- Fast proof beats: 0.25-0.55s.
- Long final hold: at least 1.2s.
- One ambient motion system only: light drift, camera float, or grain.
- No constant pulsing on every element.

## Product Image Inputs

Ask for or infer:

- Transparent product PNG preferred.
- If only JPG: use a poster card, crop, or mask.
- If image quality is low: reduce scale, add matte, shadow, and framing.
- If product is UI/software: use screen mockup plus one key screen, not many tiny screenshots.

## High-End Defaults

For a premium product launch without a brand system:

- Background: warm black or off-white, not pure black/white.
- Accent: brass, vermilion, acid green, cobalt, or a single brand hue.
- Typography: serif display + quiet sans, or condensed sans + mono.
- Layout: asymmetrical, with one edge-anchored block and one hero object.
