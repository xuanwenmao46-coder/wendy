# Image Audit

Score each image from 1-5.

## Criteria

- **Resolution**: can it survive the intended scale?
- **Lighting**: does it have believable direction and contrast?
- **Silhouette**: is the product recognizable at a glance?
- **Material**: does it show glass, metal, fabric, paper, plastic, or surface quality?
- **Era**: does it feel current, or like old clipart/stock?
- **Edges**: transparency, mask quality, antialiasing.
- **Brand fit**: premium, playful, technical, editorial, etc.
- **Composability**: can type and proof points sit near it cleanly?

## Decision

- **5**: hero-ready. Use large.
- **4**: usable with crop/light/shadow.
- **3**: downgrade to inset/card/detail.
- **2**: use only for rough prototype.
- **1**: reject.

## Common Failures

- Old 3D object at odd perspective.
- Low-res transparent PNG from aggregator sites.
- Product angle leaves no clean space for type.
- Screenshot full of tiny unreadable UI.
- Lighting contradicts the scene.
- Image has no premium material cue.
