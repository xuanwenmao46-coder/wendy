# Kinetic Type Patterns

Use these patterns when text should become the main motion actor.

## 1. Word Hit

Best for punchy slogans, hooks, section titles.

- Split: phrase into 2-5 word blocks.
- Motion: each word enters with y/scale/opacity, final word gets overshoot.
- Timing: 0.08-0.16s stagger, 0.6-1.0s total.
- Hold: keep the complete phrase still for 0.5s minimum.

## 2. Flash Stack

Best for rapid feature lists, mood words, social/ad edits.

- Split: multiple text blocks in different screen zones.
- Motion: blink in/out, scale pulse, quick mask wipe.
- Timing: each flash 0.08-0.18s, no more than 2-3 full-screen flashes per second.
- Use: create energy without requiring the viewer to read every block.

## 3. Masked Phrase Reveal

Best for premium titles and editorial motion.

- Split: line or phrase.
- Motion: static mask window, text slides through and lands.
- Timing: 0.4-0.8s.
- Detail: add a thin rule or block that travels with the mask.

## 4. Type Compression / Expansion

Best for showing pressure, speed, scale, transformation.

- Split: one short hero word or phrase.
- Motion: scaleX/letter spacing/clip reveal, then settle.
- Timing: quick compression 0.15-0.25s, release 0.35-0.6s.
- Avoid: dense Chinese sentences; use short words or English labels.

## 5. Scatter And Recompose

Best for chaos-to-order stories.

- Split: characters or word blocks.
- Motion: pieces begin offset/rotated/blurred, then assemble into final phrase.
- Timing: 0.8-1.4s.
- Rule: only use for short text; readability depends on the final landing.

## 6. Editorial Word Swap

Best for comparing concepts or changing interpretation.

- Split: one stable phrase plus one replaceable word.
- Motion: old word exits with mask/slide, new word snaps in.
- Timing: 0.25-0.5s per swap.
- Example: "做 PPT" -> "做 Motion" -> "做 Video System".

## 7. Lower Third Pop

Best for names, labels, news tags, product annotations.

- Split: tag, title, descriptor.
- Motion: bar wipes in, tag locks, title follows, descriptor fades.
- Timing: 0.4-0.9s in, 3-5s hold, 0.25-0.5s out.
- Rule: keep within title-safe margins.
