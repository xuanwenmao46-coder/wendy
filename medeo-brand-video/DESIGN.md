# Medeo Brand Motion Video Design

## Brand tokens

- Brand name: MEDEO
- Brand ID: medeo-brand-video
- Tagline: Connected Care Intelligence
- Website / CTA: medeo.health
- Year: 2026
- Founded: 2024
- Discipline: Health Technology
- Scope: Care Operating System

## Palette

- Primary: #00D7C0 (clinical signal teal)
- Dark: #071316 (near-black blue green)
- Light: #F2FFFC (sterile mint white)
- Secondary: #3B82F6 (medical blue)
- Alert accent: #B8FF2C (system pulse green)

## Typography

- Display: Impact / Arial Narrow fallback, uppercase, tight tracking.
- UI: Inter / Helvetica / Arial fallback.
- Numeric labels: tabular sans-serif, high contrast.

## Motion language

- Swiss grid construction, scan-line detection, diagonal floods, data dashboards.
- Motion should feel precise, clinical, and high-confidence rather than playful.
- The primary teal is used for signal, diagnosis, and convergence moments.

## Brand message

Medeo turns scattered clinical signals into connected, real-time care intelligence.

## Audience

- Digital health teams
- Clinical operations leaders
- Provider networks
- Care coordinators
- Patient experience teams
- Healthcare executives
